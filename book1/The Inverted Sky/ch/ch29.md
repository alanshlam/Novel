**Scene 1: The Sub-Surface Vault**

Above ground, the Capital was a city of polished mirrors and loud
performance.

On every major news feed across the sector, Vanguard Therapeutics was
broadcasting slick, high-definition press statements. Their pristine
corporate spokespeople offered warm smiles, expressing \"unwavering
commitment to absolute transparency\" and promising full cooperation
with the newly established Presidential Commission on Biological
Integrity. To the public, it was the noble response of a world-class
pharmaceutical giant eager to clear its name after a tragic, isolated
frontier crisis.

Two hundred feet beneath the downtown government quarter, inside the
reinforced subterranean vaults of the Old Federal Reserve Annex, nobody
was buying the propaganda.

The room smelled of stale coffee, ozone, and cold concrete. Thick steel
blast doors---once designed to secure the nation's gold reserves---now
sealed the PCBI Special Operations & Investigative Division inside an
air-gapped Faraday cage. On the walls, flickering holographic displays
cast a harsh blue glow over a small group of men and women who shared a
single, dangerous bond: every soldier in the room had survived the
black-budget death trap at Dunfeld, owed their lives to Colonel Sean
Walker, and bore the invisible scars of a High Command that had tried to
bury them.

Standing at the head of the tactical conference table, Colonel Sean
Walker looked across his newly authorized roster.

President Callahan had finally signed the Executive Order. The political
panel upstairs might handle the public hearings and congressional
optics, but here in the vault, Sean commanded the PCBI's actual teeth:
an FBI-style investigative and tactical division backed by presidential
pardons, full subpoena authority, and a heavily armed special protection
detail under Captain Viktor Brand to keep Dr. Elena Vance and key
witnesses alive.

Sean leaned over the tactical map, his voice quiet, steady, and devoid
of warmth. \"The public expects the PCBI to deliver the truth. Vanguard
is telling the news feeds they're ready to cooperate, which means
they've already hidden the bodies and scrubbed the paper trails. Let\'s
see what we actually have. Fedor, start us off. What did you pull on

## Scene 2: The Race for Evidence

The heavy armored personnel carrier rattled over the rain-slick pavement
of the Capital's outer district. Inside the vehicle's reinforced command
compartment, the hum of tactical displays was punctuated by the low
thrum of military-grade signal dampeners.

Captain Viktor Brand sat near the rear ramp, his tactical harness
strapped tight, eyes scanning the perimeter telemetry through his helmet
display. Opposite him, Colonel Sean Walker and Dr. Elena Vance leaned
over a glowing holoscope interface.

The comms console chimed, and Lieutenant Anya Petrova's voice cut
through the speaker, crisp and urgent.

*\"Colonel, I just cross-referenced the registry we subpoenaed from the
central distribution clinic against Vanguard's satellite fleet
movement,\"* Anya reported. *\"It's bad news. Vanguard field teams
visited every recipient on that list over the last seventy-two
hours---before their corporate lawyers finally handed the registry over
to Counselor Cross.\"*

Sean's jaw tightened. \"They stalled the legal request just long enough
to visit the witnesses first,\" he said, turning to Elena. \"They're
running circles around the paperwork.\"

\"Then we need to know exactly what they did to those people,\" Elena
said, her analytical eyes fixed on the display.

Sean tapped the comms link. \"Anya, pull up the very last recipient
Vanguard visited. We\'re rerouting.\"

*\"Copy that, Colonel. Target is Nurse Maya Lin, former frontline medic
stationed in the Sirona border sector. Current address is four blocks
from your location.\"*

Ten minutes later, the APC idling in the alley below, Sean and Elena
stood inside the modest apartment of Nurse Maya Lin. Captain Brand
remained stationed at the doorway, sidearm drawn in a low-ready stance,
his eyes sweeping the hallway for Vanguard surveillance units.

The nurse sat on the edge of her sofa, clutching a mug of tea with
trembling hands while Elena gently secured a portable scanner around her
arm, drawing a fresh vial of blood.

\"They came three days ago,\" Maya whispered, looking up at Sean's
immaculate military tunic. \"Two men in unmarked white scrubs with
Vanguard corporate badges. They said it was a mandatory safety
protocol---a booster for the **Respira-Vax VTX-709** we were given in
Sirona.\"

\"Did they inject you?\" Sean asked smoothly, keeping his tone calm.
\"Did they use any equipment?\"

\"An injection in the arm, yes,\" Maya nodded. \"But then they put a
rigid metal collar around my neck and a heavy bracelet on my wrist. They
told me it was a real-time biometric diagnostic tool to monitor my
body\'s reaction to the booster.\"

Elena glanced sharply at Sean. *A near-field electromagnetic pulse
generator,* her eyes said. *A localized disassembly trigger.*

\"How did you feel right after they turned it on?\" Elena asked softly,
sitting beside her.

\" Terrifying for the first half-hour,\" Maya admitted, shivering. \"It
started with a sudden, prickling heat deep in my chest and the back of
my neck. My head was spinning\... and I had this awful, bitter metallic
taste in my mouth, like chewing on copper wire. My skin went bright
red.\"

\"And after they left?\"

\"The next twenty-four hours were miserable. I couldn\'t stop drinking
water. I had severe stomach cramps, drenching cold sweats, and my
urine\... it turned dark amber. Almost like black tea. I thought my
organs were failing.\" Maya paused, taking a breath. \"But then
yesterday morning\... I woke up, and it was completely gone. The aches
vanished. My energy bounced right back. I feel totally fine now.\"

Sean stepped forward. \"Thank you, Nurse Lin. Stay inside, keep your
doors locked, and don\'t speak to anyone from Vanguard.\"

Back inside the armored carrier, the heavy rear ramp slammed shut,
sealing them back inside the Faraday-shielded vault of the vehicle.

Elena immediately slotted the blood sample into the integrated
laboratory analyzer. The holoscope chimed, projecting a
three-dimensional cellular matrix into the air between them.
High-resolution bio-forensic graphs flashed in urgent amber and red
warnings.

Elena stared at the floating data, her breath catching. \"They aren\'t
trying to hide the nanobots, Sean,\" she said, her voice dropping into a
shocked whisper. \"They're *purging* them.\"

\"Break it down for me, Elena,\" Sean commanded, leaning over the
console.

\"Look at her kidney filtration logs,\" Elena pointed to a spiking curve
on the holographic display. \"When near-field magnetic nanobots break
down, they fragment into microscopic carbon-fullerenes and lipids.
Maya's glomerular filtration rate spiked to emergency levels over the
last twenty-four hours. Her kidneys were working at two hundred percent
capacity to clear kilograms of microscopic synthetic debris from her
bloodstream. That's what caused the dark, tea-colored urine.\"

She zoomed in on a microscopic cluster of deep blue blood cells.

\"And here is the smoking gun,\" Elena continued, her tone sharpening
with cold, scientific conviction. \"Look at these macrophages---her
white blood cells. They're completely gorged on iron-oxide residue. Her
serum ferritin---stored iron---is sitting at eight hundred percent above
normal physiological limits. That isn\'t dietary, and it isn\'t a
natural virus. Those are the dissolved metallic cores of the
micro-transceivers. It's why she tasted metal in her mouth.\"

Sean's eyes hardened into ice. \"Vanguard melted the nanobots inside her
veins,\" he said grimly, \"and let her flush the hardware down the
toilet.\"

\"Exactly,\" Elena said. \"Without active hardware controlling her
system, her bone marrow resumed normal leukocyte production. To a
civilian doctor, it looks like a miraculous recovery. But
biologically\... the evidence of the purge is still sitting in her
blood.\"

Before Sean could respond, the console screen flickered, and Major
Dimitri Sidorov's face appeared on the secondary monitor.

*\"Colonel, we have an emergency movement,\"* Dimitri reported fast.
*\"Vanguard just pulled three hundred thousand untouched doses of
VTX-709 out of the Sirona war zone reserves. They just trucked them into
the Vanguard Central Cryo-Storage & Distribution Facility on the city
limits.\"*

\"Are they trying to destroy the unissued batches?\" Sean asked.

*\"That's their plan,\"* Dimitri confirmed. *\"Major Leo Vance is on
site right now with a PCBI legal enforcement detail trying to serve the
presidential seizure warrant. But Vanguard's private corporate security
forces have locked down the perimeter. They're refusing entry and
threatening to use lethal force.\"*

Sean turned to Captain Brand, whose hand was already on his assault
rifle\'s safety switch.

\"Brand, hit the sirens and override the traffic grids,\" Sean ordered,
his voice echoing through the steel interior with absolute authority.
\"Tell Leo to hold his line. We'll be at the Cryo-Facility in thirty
minutes.\"

## Scene 3: Fire and Vapor

The security gates of the Vanguard Central Cryo-Storage Facility looked
less like a commercial warehouse and more like a fortress siege.

Two heavily armed forces stood bumper-to-bumper in the freezing rain,
rifles leveled across hood bonnets. On one side stood Major Leo Vance
and his PCBI federal enforcement team. On the other stood forty private
corporate mercenaries wearing matte-black tactical gear, backed by
automated defense turrets mounted along the loading bay roof. Behind the
mercenary line, towering stacks of vacuum-sealed climate pallets sat
ready for destruction---containing three hundred thousand untouched
doses of **VTX-709** recalled from Sirona.

Sean's heavy armored personnel carrier slammed to a halt in the middle
of the standoff, its hydraulic ramp dropping with a metallic clang.
Sean, Dr. Elena Vance, and Captain Viktor Brand stepped into the rain,
flanked by Brand's assault squad.

Sean strode straight to Leo's command position. \"Report, Leo.\"

\"Complete legal brick wall,\" Leo said, his uniform soaked, eyes fixed
on the mercenary line. \"Their legal team is claiming absolute
intellectual property protection. They\'re asserting that under federal
defense contract laws, the VTX-709 batches are proprietary biological
assets. They claim PCBI seizure violates corporate IP immunity.\"

\"Victoria tried calling Vanguard HQ directly,\" Leo added, nodding
toward Counselor Cross, who was frantically tapping at her encrypted
terminal. \"Every corporate landline and legal server at Vanguard went
dark five minutes ago.\"

\"Convenient,\" Sean growled. He stepped out past the armored hood,
raising his hands slightly in a non-threatening posture as he locked
eyes with the towering mercenary chief standing at the center of the
Vanguard perimeter. \"Commander! I'm Colonel Sean Walker, Director of
Field Operations for the PCBI. There's a presidential warrant for those
pallets. Call your corporate liaison.\"

The security chief didn\'t flinch. His visor was polarized, reflecting
Sean's image in cold silver. \"I take my orders from Vanguard Asset
Protection, Colonel. Standard protocol for high-value biologicals. Until
corporate overrides my directive, no unauthorized government personnel
step across this yellow perimeter line.\"

\"Your corporate superiors are currently obstructing a federal
bioweapons investigation,\" Sean warned, his voice dropping into a
razor-sharp register. \"If you or your men tamper with those vaccines,
you will be prosecuted as co-conspirators in high treason. Call your
superior officer right now and tell him President Callahan's signature
is on this warrant.\"

The security chief tapped his helmet comms, listened for two quiet
seconds, and looked back at Sean. \"I just spoke with my Director. His
orders are absolute: *Hold the line.*\"

He raised his hand, and forty assault rifles clicked into full-automatic
locks.

\"You have ten seconds to clear this tarmac, Colonel,\" the chief said
coldly. \"After that, my contract authorizes lethal force to prevent the
breach of proprietary assets.\"

Inside the command APC, a sudden, frantic electronic warble screamed
from the environmental sensor console. Captain Brand's head snapped up.

*\"COLONEL! MAJOR! DR. VANCE!\"* Brand bellowed over the vehicle\'s
external speakers, his voice raw with sudden panic. *\"INTO THE APC!
NOW!\"*

Before Sean could question the order, Brand sprinted out, grabbed Elena
by her tactical vest, and threw her up the rear ramp into the armored
carrier. He shoved Sean and Leo inside right behind her.

*\"GET THE DOORS CLOSED! MOVE! MOVE!\"* Brand roared into the driver's
cab.

\"Brand, what is it?!\" Sean shouted, grabbing a handrail as the heavy
steel ramp slammed shut and sealed.

Brand floored the accelerator. The massive twelve-ton armored vehicle
lurked forward with a roar, smashing straight through a concrete
security barrier and tearing through the outer chain-link perimeter
fence, leaving the confused Vanguard mercenaries behind in a cloud of
diesel smoke.

\"Facility telemetry just spiked!\" Brand shouted over the engine roar,
spinning the wheel as the APC fishtailed onto the main highway.
\"Concentrated anhydrous ammonia leaking from the subterranean
cryo-coolers! It's over-pressurizing the primary heat exchangers!\"

Elena's face turned deathly pale. \"Anhydrous ammonia\... at those
storage volumes, an over-pressurization isn\'t an accident---\"

*\"IT'S A THERMOBARIC BOMB!\"* Brand yelled.

A split second later, the universe behind them turned blinding white.

Even inside the heavily insulated, lead-lined hull of the APC, the
shockwave hit like a physical fist. The twelve-ton vehicle was lifted
two feet off its rear tires, skidding sideways across three lanes of
asphalt as a roaring shockwave shattered every window for a mile in
every direction.

Sean looked into the rear tactical optic display. Where the Vanguard
Cryo-Storage Facility had stood moments before, a churning mushroom
cloud of orange fire and black chemical smoke was rolling thousands of
feet into the rain-heavy sky.

The three hundred thousand doses of VTX-709---and forty Vanguard
mercenaries---had been vaporized in a single, roaring flash.
